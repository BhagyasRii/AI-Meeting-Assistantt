About the Project

Built to solve a real problem

A capstone multi-modal AI system for intelligent document and speech summarization with interactive query answering — developed under Section C, Batch 08.

The Problem We Solved

Teams lose hours every week re-reading long transcripts, chasing follow-ups, and trying to recall what was actually decided. Critical context gets buried, action items are forgotten, and decision rationale is lost.

Our system automatically extracts the signal from the noise — giving every team member a clear, structured record of what happened and what needs to happen next.
